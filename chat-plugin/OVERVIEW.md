Chat lets the people who share one Paseo daemon message each other, lets your agents message people for you, and gives you an AI Assistant that answers what others send you while you are away.

## What you get

- **Chat screen.** **Chat** in the sidebar (or ⌘K → **Open Chat**). Four columns: **AI** (your AI Assistant and the workers answering for you), **Cần lưu ý** (what you must not miss), your conversations with unread counts (`@` when you are mentioned), and everyone in chat with their presence. Pick a person to message them, or **Nhóm** to start a group of up to ten.
- **Project rooms.** Every project has one room, `<project>-chung`. The chat icon in a workspace header opens the Chat panel beside your work, with four tabs: **Thành viên**, **Tin nhắn**, **Nhóm**, **Cần lưu ý**.
- **Conversations as tabs.** A conversation opens as a tab with Paseo's own composer: Markdown, up to 4000 characters, up to four images of 10 MB. **Nhắc tên** mentions someone; **Khẩn** marks a message urgent.
- **Presence.** A dot on each avatar: green **Đang hoạt động** (input in the last 5 minutes), amber **AFK**, grey **Offline**, plus how many of the person's agents are working.
- **Notifications.** An urgent message sent to you or mentioning you opens a window over your work, with a reply box. Normal direct messages and mentions show a toast. **Settings → Workspace Chat** chooses what shows a toast and turns the window off.
- **Tools for your agents.** Every agent can list people (`chat_people`), read and search your conversations, see their images (`chat_image`), and send a message as you, images included (`chat_send`). Others see it as your message, marked as sent through an agent. A message an agent sends waiting for replies opens a request: answers come back to that agent for 24 hours, and while the request is open for up to 14 days, and each reply can say where the request stands (working, needs input, done, failed, canceled). Agents also register signals and subscribe to them (`chat_signal_register`, `chat_signal_subscribe`, `chat_signal_unsubscribe`, `chat_signal_list`): the chat broker pulls Grafana every 30 seconds when it has GRAFANA_URL, and the alerts whose rule carries the label `signal=<name>` land, grouped per poll, in the room `signals/<name>` and reach the subscribed agent, or your AI Assistant once that agent is gone. Chat settings list every signal and who subscribes.
- **AI Assistant.** Always on. It answers direct messages and mentions for you: a **Router** tab hands each piece of work to a **worker** tab that keeps its context, and the worker replies marked as sent by **AI Assistant**. Each worker reports its checklist to the router, which follows up work that stalls. A worker whose checklist is all done, with nothing waiting or blocked, is archived after 5 idle minutes; new messages about the same work wake it with its context. It works with your full rights, guided by your **persona**: plain files of your rules and taste in `~/.paseo/chat/<handle>/`. Shape it in the **Talk to myself** tab, which also improves the persona from your chats and Claude sessions once a day.
- **Cần lưu ý.** The assistant notes todos asked of you, events with a time and decisions from your chats, and **AI bị vướng**: a worker's work only you can settle, after its router could not. Its screen puts each kind in its own column; picking one opens the agent that noted it, or the stuck worker; a row's **⋯** menu forks that agent into a new tab to talk on about the item, marks it done or removes it.

## How it works

Messages, rooms and images live at the chat broker on the same machine. Each client picks who is at it (**Bạn là ai?**) and the daemon acts as that person; an agent acts for the owner of its workspace (**Nhận workspace này** in the Chat panel). Each person's assistant state (workers, the Cần lưu ý list, the persona) is under `~/.paseo/chat/<handle>/`. Nothing reaches a person whose Paseo is closed; they see the unread count next time.
